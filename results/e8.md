# E8 results

E8 gives the model the recognition test used to measure hippocampal function in people, the Mnemonic Similarity Task, built from Omniglot drawings. The model studies a list of drawings once, then sees repeats (the same drawing), lures (another person's drawing of a studied character) and foils (characters it never studied), and answers old, similar or new. Its two decision thresholds were set on background alphabets so that it calls 81.2% of repeats old and 82.0% of foils new, as young adults do; everything else is a prediction. The protocol, with every choice and free parameter, is `docs/e8_protocol.md`; the human findings and their sources are `docs/e8_sources.md`; the numbers come from `results/e8_*.json`.

**Main result.** At 20 studied drawings the fully local hippocampus tells lures from studied drawings about as often as young adults overall (lure discrimination index 0.282 against 0.360), but by the opposite route. It never calls a lure old, where people call 40.3% of lures old. Its discrimination is highest for the lures closest to the studied drawing and falls below zero for the least similar ones, which it calls new. All six predictions written before the runs failed. The vision code alone, with no hippocampus, discriminates better (0.422). Thinning the perforant path lowers lure discrimination step by step while recognition stays near intact, the pattern seen with age, but through fewer lures being completed to the studied memory, not more.

## Terms

- **LDI**, the lure discrimination index: p(similar | lure) − p(similar | foil). **REC**, the recognition score: p(old | repeat) − p(old | foil). Young adults score 0.360 and 0.786 (Stark and colleagues 2013, Table 2).
- **Signal**: the cosine between the test drawing's EC code (its vision code) and the EC code CA1 returns after the perforant path cues CA3 and CA3 settles. Above θ_old = 0.968 the model answers old, below θ_new = 0.503 new, between them similar.
- **Lure bins**: five equal-count bins by the cosine between a lure's EC code and its studied drawing's, L1 most similar. Edges were set on background lures.
- **Settling onto the studied pattern**: CA3's final state is exactly the stored pattern of the drawing the lure was made from. This is pattern completion of the lure to the studied memory.
- **Fixed thresholds**: every lesion, load and interference condition uses the intact model's thresholds. Each arm's own thresholds, recalibrated on its background runs, are a secondary check.

Values are means over 10 seeds ± one standard deviation; each seed runs about 1,000 studied drawings.

## What the numbers show

- **The model answers by how close a lure looks, and people answer by whether it brings back the memory.** A lure's signal can reach at most its cosine with the studied drawing, since the best CA1 can do is return that drawing's code. Omniglot lures sit far below the old threshold, so lures come out similar when they are close and new when they are not. People call the closest lures old and the farthest similar. p(similar | lure) falls from 74.3% in L1 to 15.3% in L5 for the intact model.
- **Completing a lure makes the model say similar, not old.** 38.7% of lures settle exactly onto the studied drawing's pattern. Of those, 84.5% are called similar and none old; of lures that settle elsewhere, 31.5% are called similar. CA1 returns the studied drawing, and the comparison with what is in front of the model shows a difference. In people, completion of a lure to the studied memory is the usual account of calling it old.
- **Settling sharpens the comparison.** Without CA3's recurrent connections, the cue reaches CA1 as a mix of memories, CA1 returns a blend that resembles any drawing a little, and the model calls 47.5% of foils similar against 23.8% with settling. LDI drops from 0.282 to 0.252 (lower in 8 of 10 seeds); REC is unchanged.
- **The vision code alone discriminates better than the hippocampus.** It calls every lure in L1 and L2 similar and almost none in L4 and L5: its signal is the lure's similarity itself. Like the intact model, it calls no lure old.
- **Removing DG halves discrimination, as in the dentate gyrus patient, but also wipes out recognition of repeats.** Without DG, similar drawings get similar CA3 patterns, 13.2% of foils settle onto a stored pattern against 90.4% intact, and only 4.2% of lures settle onto the studied pattern. Repeats still settle onto their own pattern (90.1%), but CA1's returned code matches them less closely: on background drawings the lowest 18.8% of repeat signals lie below 0.746 without DG, against 0.968 intact, and the model calls 95.0% of evaluation repeats similar. The patient's recognition was normal (Stark and colleagues 2019), so with fixed thresholds the match holds for LDI only.
- **Thinning the perforant path follows the ageing pattern in LDI and REC.** Adults aged 60 to 74 score LDI 0.113 and REC 0.774, against 0.360 and 0.786 for young adults (Stark and colleagues 2013, Table 2); the model with 25% of connections kept scores 0.180 and 0.777. Older people call more lures old (54.6% against 40.3%). The thinned model instead completes fewer lures and calls more of them new (65.1% at 25% kept, against 48.0% intact).
- **Half of CA1 lowers recognition but not discrimination.** REC falls from 0.797 to 0.717 (lower in 10 of 10 seeds) and LDI stays at 0.291. In transient global amnesia, with lesions in CA1, both fell (Hanert and colleagues 2019).
- **Removing CA3 and keeping only EC's direct input to CA1** leaves LDI at 0.252 and raises REC to 0.875. For a repeat, the direct drive picks exactly the CA1 cells that plateaued at study, so 87.5% of repeats are called old.
- **Without the hippocampus the model calls everything new.** People with hippocampal damage kept recognition near normal on an old/new test (0.81 against 0.87 for controls) while their discrimination fell (0.10 against 0.37; Kirwan and colleagues 2012). The model's only memory outside the hippocampus is the vision-code baseline, and it discriminates better than the intact model, the reverse of the patients.
- **Recognition collapses by 50 drawings.** The median signal for repeats falls from 0.991 at 20 drawings to 0.875 at 50, 0.797 at 100 and 0.688 at 200, below the old threshold set at 20, so p(old | repeat) falls to 6.2% at 50. CA1's returned code blends more studied drawings as the list grows. The vision-code baseline keeps REC at 0.614 at 50 and 0.506 at 200.
- **Several drawings of one character raise discrimination.** With 2 and 4 studied drawings per character, LDI rises to 0.415 and 0.587 and REC falls to 0.773 and 0.696. A lure then resembles more studied drawings and is more often called similar (64.3% and 79.4%), while foils barely change (22.8% and 20.7% called similar, against 23.8%).
